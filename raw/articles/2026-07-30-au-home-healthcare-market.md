Australia home healthcare market size reached USD 12.9 billion in 2025. Projected to reach USD 28.0 billion by 2034 at a CAGR of 8.71%. Drivers: aging population, chronic disease prevalence, shift from hospital to home-based care models. The workforce shortage in home care is estimated to reach 140,000 by 2030, partly due to financial challenges and low wages.
