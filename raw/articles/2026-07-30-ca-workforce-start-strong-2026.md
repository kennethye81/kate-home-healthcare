Demand is rising across hospitals, clinics, and long-term care homes in Canada. Employers are looking for trained professionals. The healthcare workforce is facing an aging workforce nearing retirement, nursing and physician shortages, and competition for temporary clinical talent. In 2026, competition for temporary clinical talent intensifies, forcing healthcare organizations to adopt more sophisticated loyalty-based rewards programs.
