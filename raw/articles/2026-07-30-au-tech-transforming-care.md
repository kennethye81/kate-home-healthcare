The mobile healthcare toolkit in 2026 has transformed significantly. Practitioners are equipped with tablets running full clinical software, portable ultrasound and ECG devices, digital otoscopes, and audiometry kits. CareMonitor is a digital healthcare platform used across Australia to support Hospital in the Home, virtual care and remote patient monitoring. Hospital capacity constraints are driving a shift to home-based remote patient monitoring (RPM). The Digital Health Blueprint supports this transition. Australia patient monitoring market: USD 1.4 billion in 2025, projected USD 3.3 billion by 2034 at CAGR of 9.22%.
