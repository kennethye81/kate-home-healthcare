The new Aged Care Act started on 1 November 2025 and responds to issues facing older people, aged care providers, workers and the broader sector. It is a rights-based legislative framework that replaces the Aged Care Act 1997. Key provisions: legally enforceable rights for care recipients, strengthened quality standards, new regulatory powers, and the Support at Home program framework.
