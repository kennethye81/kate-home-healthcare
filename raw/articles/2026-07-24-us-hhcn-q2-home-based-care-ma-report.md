# Fewer Deals, Big Checks: Scaling Platforms Lead Q2 Home-Based Care M&A

**Source:** Home Health Care News (HHCN)
**Author:** Morgan Gonzales
**Date:** July 2026
**Key Themes:** M&A, home-based care, private equity, valuation

**Key data from Mertz Taggart's Q2 2026 Home-Based Care M&A Report:**

- **Q2 2026 deal volume:** 16 closed transactions (down from 27 in Q1)
- **Largest transactions:**
  - General Atlantic acquired TEAM Services Group for $3B (April 2026)
  - Kinderhook Industries acquired Enhabit for $1.1B (May 2026)
- **Enhabit valuation:** 10.2x EBITDA on $108M EBITDA; 24% premium to undisturbed share price; nearly 34% premium to 60-day average

**Deal composition (reversal of multi-year trend — platform deals > add-ons):**
- 6 new private equity platform deals
- 4 sponsor-backed strategic add-ons
- 1 public-company acquisition
- 5 post-acute or independent buyers

**Skill home health:** 6 deals closed in Q2, down from 8 in prior quarters

**Non-medical home care:** 8 deals closed, including TEAM Services Group. Other notable deals:
- Warburg Pincus platform investment in Cornerstone Caregiving
- Addus HomeCare (Nasdaq: ADUS) acquired HomeCourt Home Care
- Care Advantage acquired First Priority Home Care

**Quote from Cory Mertz (Mertz Taggart):** "The count came down this quarter, and it's fair to ask whether the regulatory environment is part of it — the fraud takedowns, the hospice 36-month rule, the new enrollment moratorium and enhanced oversight all make deals more complex to get across the line."

**Quote:** "Diligence around billing and compliance has only intensified — especially in the enhanced-oversight states — and the sellers who invest early in getting their house in order are the ones who hold their value all the way through to close."
