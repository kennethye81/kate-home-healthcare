# NHS England Virtual Wards Operational Framework (PRN01289)

**Source:** NHS England
**Published:** 2024/2025 (framework updates 2 previous guidance documents)

## Summary
Official NHS England operational framework for virtual wards (also known as hospital at home services). Provides national consistency guidance for virtual wards across all 42 ICSs. Replaces previous 'Supporting information for ICS leads' documents.

## Key Points
- **Core expectation:** Maintain virtual ward capacity with occupancy consistently above 80%
- **Clinical support:** Broad support from professional bodies; growing evidence base for patient, system, and staff benefits
- **Admissions avoidance:** South East evaluation of 22,000 virtual ward admissions found 2.5 virtual ward admissions = 1 fewer non-elective admission (9,000 avoided admissions/year across region). Most mature services achieve nearly 1:1 ratio
- **Frailty HaH:** Kent Community Health NHS FT - over 2 years of HaH expansion, medical hospital admissions of 75+ population reduced by 25%
- **Cost-effectiveness:** Step-down virtual ward models reduce length of stay for COPD and heart failure cohorts
- **Children & Young People:** PATCH in Hillingdon showed significantly reduced paediatric assessment unit admissions
- **Legal framework:** UK GDPR + Data Protection Act 2018 + UK Medical Devices Regulations 2002 + Medicines and Medical Devices Act 2021
- **Vendor compliance:** Data Security and Protection Toolkit (DSPT) + NHS Digital Clinical Safety Standards (DCB0129/DCB0160)
- **CQC registration:** Required for clinical providers; ongoing MHRA Software Group oversight for AI/software devices
