The Support at Home Program is a new Australian Government aged care program designed to replace Home Care Packages from 1 July 2025. It forms part of the Federal Government's response to the Royal Commission into Aged Care Quality and Safety. The program consolidates existing home care and short-term support programmes into a single, more flexible funding model. Key features: (1) Replaces the old 4-level Home Care Packages (HCP) and Short-Term Restorative Care (STRC) programs; (2) Built on the new Aged Care Act 2024 which commenced 1 November 2025; (3) Creates legally enforceable rights for all older Australians in government-funded care; (4) Establishes mandatory staffing standards; (5) Restructures home care through a unified program.
