# Navigating the 2026 Home Health & Hospice M&A Market

**Source:** Levin Associates
**Author:** Dylan Sammut
**Date:** July 2, 2026
**Key Themes:** M&A, home health, hospice, reimbursement, regulatory

**M&A volume (H1 2026):** 55 Home Health & Hospice transactions (vs 58 in H1 2025)
- 41% of deals committed by PE firms
- High-profile platform deals: Palladium Equity Partners, Warburg Pincus added new home health operators
- Largest deal: General Atlantic acquired TEAM Services Group from Alpine Investors for $3B (incl. debt)
- Kinderhook Industries acquired Enhabit for $1.1B all-cash (take-private from NYSE)

**Reimbursement environment: "Disruption"**
- CMS final rule for 2026 HH PPS: payments reduced by aggregate 1.3% (~$220M) vs 2025
- Includes temporary 3% cut to recover ~$4.76B in overpayments from 2020–2024
- Addus HomeCare and BrightSpring noted changes are manageable at scale but caution remains

**CMS nationwide six-month enrollment moratorium:**
- Pauses enrollment of new Medicare home health agencies and hospices to combat fraud
- Blocks re-enrollment of agencies undergoing non-exempt change in majority ownership (CIMO) within 36 months
- Forces dealmakers to restructure transactions, extends deal timelines
- Pushes more into M&A market (organic/de novo expansion effectively frozen)

**Tech-enabled care trends:**
- Danaher acquired Masimo for $9.9B (4.7x annual revenue) — continuous monitoring wearables
- Integrated Home Care Services acquired Dina Care (care-at-home network management platform)
- Harbor Health acquired Rippl (dementia home care platform)
- Caregiver turnover at 75% — every state reports a worker shortage
- AI seen as solution for documentation automation, scheduling optimization, care coordination
