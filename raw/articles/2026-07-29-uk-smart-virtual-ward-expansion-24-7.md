# Virtual Ward Expansion Shows How Home Care Technology Is Reshaping NHS Recovery

**Source:** Innovative Care NEWS
**Published:** 2026-05-07

## Summary
The Surgical, Medical and Acute Recovery Team (SMART) virtual ward service has expanded from 80 to 120 virtual beds and now operates 24/7. Patients monitored via digital devices alongside video consultations and telephone support.

## Key Data
- **Capacity increase:** 80 → 120 virtual beds
- **Operating model:** 24/7 round-the-clock service
- **Average time under care:** Reduced from 9 to 8 days
- **Technology:** Remote monitoring devices (blood pressure, oxygen saturation, pulse, temperature) + portable elastomeric pumps for IV antibiotics
- **Patient quote:** Elliot Hall (Gillingham) - recovery at home improved wellbeing and confidence
- **Future target:** 200-bed virtual hospital later in 2026
- **Director quote (Tracy Stocker):** "Research shows that patients recover quicker and better in familiar surroundings"

## Significance
Extension to 24/7 allows clinicians to safely manage patients with higher acuity needs outside traditional hospital environments. The hybrid model (remote monitoring + mobile clinical support) reflects broader trend toward community health technology.
