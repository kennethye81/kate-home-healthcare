# SCHSA launches "Smart Accident Detection System and Service" pilot scheme

**Source:** Senior Citizen Home Safety Association, March 9, 2026

**Key Content:**
- Pilot scheme: 500 elderly households (300 funded by LWB + HKEX Foundation, 200 by Solar Foundation + Tsang Man Dat Charitable Fund)
- 24 months free detection and support services
- "Smart AI fall detector": uses mm-wave detection + AI analysis, no photography/recording (privacy protected)
- 4G communication, no WiFi required, two-way real-time conversation
- Upon fall detection: auto-connects to Care-on-Call 24-hour Service Centre
- Also detects prolonged inactivity (e.g., not leaving living room for 24+ hours)
- Case example: 81-year-old Mrs Ng who fell at 5am, unconscious for 2 hours
- SCHSA approaching 30th anniversary; evolving from "Safety Guardianship" to "Proactive Detection"
