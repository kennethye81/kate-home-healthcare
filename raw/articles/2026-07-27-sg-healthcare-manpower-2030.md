# Healthcare manpower will be key agenda in coming years: Ong Ye Kung

**Source:** The Straits Times, January 16, 2026
**Author:** Zhaki Abdullah

**Key Content:**
- Healthcare manpower is a "key agenda" for Singapore
- National healthcare workforce must grow by **20% by 2030** to support new facilities (Eastern General Hospital opening 2029)
- "Nesting" strategy: new hospital staff deployed to existing facilities first for training
- Healthcare roles being redesigned: expanded optometrist roles, community care job redesign
- Flexible modular work-study training programmes for nurses and allied health professionals
- Zero-tolerance policy for abuse against healthcare workers
- 5,171 awards presented at Singapore Health Quality Service Awards to 49 institutions
