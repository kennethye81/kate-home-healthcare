The Government of Canada introduced legislation in February 2026 to build a more connected health care system by promoting unobstructed access to electronic health information. The Connected Care for Canadians Act aims to create a national standard for health information interoperability. While the Act is federal legislation, individual provinces and hospital health networks may develop more stringent requirements. The Act addresses a key barrier to home care and hospital-at-home expansion: seamless data sharing across care settings.
