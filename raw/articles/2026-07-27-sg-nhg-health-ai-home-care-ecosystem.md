# Healthcare without walls: How NHG Health is building a scalable, AI-enabled home care ecosystem

**Source:** Hospital Management Asia, 2026
**Authors:** Sangeetha Nair (interview with Dr Ravi Sachdev, Chief Clinical Informatics Officer, Tan Tock Seng Hospital / NHG)

**Key Content:**
- Dr Ravi Sachdev leading telehealth and home care across NHG (National Healthcare Group)
- **AI triage:** Algorithms identify which remote monitoring readings are abnormal enough to need intervention
- **One-week telehealth pathway:** GP referrals to specialists via telepods — reduce 6-8 week wait to same week
- **Telepods:** AI-enabled tools including RPPG (Remote Photoplethysmography) sensors, peripheral devices, at GP practices
- **AI-assisted auscultation:** Digital stethoscopes with AI interpretation of heart/lung sounds at home
- **Device-free monitoring:** Using patient's mobile phone camera for RPPG-based vital signs (BP, HR, oxygenation)
- **Consumer wearables:** Validating smart watches for medical-grade monitoring
- Philosophy: "Clinicians cannot confine to standard consult — data from phones, wearables, home devices must be used"
