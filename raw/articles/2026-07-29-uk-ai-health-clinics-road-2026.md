# AI in UK Health Clinics: The Road to 2026

**Source:** Japeto Labs
**Published:** 2026

## Summary
Comprehensive overview of AI adoption trajectory in UK healthcare, from 2019 NHS AI Lab to 2026.

## Key Milestones
- **2019:** NHS AI Lab launched (£250M investment)
- **2022:** CQC published review of ML applications in diagnostic services
- **2024:** £21M AI Diagnostic Fund (AIDF); MHRA + NHS AI Lab launched AI Airlock regulatory sandbox; 76% NHS staff support AI for patient care (Health Foundation survey)
- **2025:** AI adoption jumped from 47% to 94% (SOTI report); 52% using AI for diagnosis, 57% for personalised treatments

## Doccla Virtual Ward AI Results (2026)
A company providing virtual ward technology to NHS Trusts reported its AI-backed model using machine learning to identify patients at risk of deterioration via clinical-grade wearables resulted in:
- **61% reduction** in bed days
- **89% reduction** in GP appointments
- **39% drop** in non-elective admissions

## AI Scribing (Ambient Voice Technology)
- Rapid spread of AVT tools for transcribing/summarising GP appointments
- Government committed to rolling out "validated AI diagnostic tools" and "AI administrative tools" NHS-wide within 2 years
- NHS England published guidance for CIOs on implementing ambient scribing products

## Public Attitudes (2026 Health Foundation survey)
Cautious but growing acceptance, highest where perceived risks are lowest.
