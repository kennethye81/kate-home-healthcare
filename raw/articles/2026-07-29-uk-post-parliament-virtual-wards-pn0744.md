# POST Parliament Research Briefing PN-0744: Virtual Wards and Hospital at Home

**Source:** UK Parliamentary Office of Science and Technology (POST)
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.58248/PN744
**Published:** 2026

## Summary
POST research briefing examines the opportunities and risks of virtual wards (hospital at home) for patients, carers, and the NHS. Covers policy considerations including staff training needs, ensuring patient safety, data and technology governance, and regulating unintended variation between virtual wards.

## Key Points
- Virtual wards allow patients to receive hospital-level care at home
- Staff training needs and patient safety are core considerations
- Data and technology governance frameworks needed
- Risk of variation between virtual wards requires regulation
