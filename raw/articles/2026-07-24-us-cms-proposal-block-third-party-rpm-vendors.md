# CMS Proposal To Block Third-Party Vendors Will Upend Remote Monitoring Services, Health Tech Leaders Say

**Source:** J&R Report / Word & Brown (original: Fierce Healthcare)
**Date:** July 21, 2026
**Key Themes:** RPM, CMS, reimbursement, third-party vendors, telehealth policy

**The proposal:** CMS issued Medicare Part B payment policy changes under the CY 2027 Physician Fee Schedule draft rule, proposing to only allow payment for RPM or RTM services when furnished by clinical staff employed by the practice — effective January 1, 2027.

**Key change:** RPM and RTM codes could not be billed when services are contracted out to third-party companies.

**Rationale:** CMS cites program integrity concerns and a September 2024 HHS OIG report finding that ~43% of enrollees receiving RPM did not receive all three required components.

**RPM payment growth:**
- 2023: $408M → 2024: $536M (+31%)
- Nearly 1M Medicare enrollees received RPM in 2024 (+27% YoY)

**Industry reaction:**
- Christopher Adamec (Alliance for Connected Care): "A dramatic reduction in unnecessary hospitalizations... many Medicare beneficiaries will lose access to services that are keeping them out of the hospital right now"
- Many providers (especially small and rural) cannot build RPM programs in-house by Jan 1, 2027 deadline
- RPM vendors exist because these capabilities are too difficult for most providers to build independently

**Cadence (RPM provider with 20+ health systems, 100K+ active patients):**
- Mayo Clinic study: Cadence model drove 27% reduction in hospital admissions, $1,302 per-patient annual cost reduction
- JACC study: 70% improvement in blood pressure control for hypertension patients
- Argues a "blanket direct-employment requirement would make it harder for even the country's largest health systems to deliver RPM at scale"

**Paradox:** The RPM policy change contrasts with recent expansion moves — CMS expanded RPM billing last year, Congress enacted $50B Rural Health Transformation Program supporting RPM, and House Ways & Means advanced the Rural Patient Monitoring Access Act.

**Other RPM changes in draft rule:**
- RTM services can only be furnished to established patients
- Providers must see patients in a separately reportable initiating visit before billing RPM/RTM
