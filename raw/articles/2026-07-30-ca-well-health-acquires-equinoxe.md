WELL Health Technologies Corp. acquired Equinoxe, Life Care Solutions Inc. — a Quebec-based provider of personalized home care primarily serving individuals with chronic diseases, post-acute needs, and home-based support. This transaction signals growing strategic interest in Canadian home care assets from public health technology companies. MNP Healthcare Services Quarterly Update Q1 2026 confirms home care is an active M&A subsector in Canada.
