# More patients in Singapore to receive hospital-level care at home and in nursing homes

**Source:** CNA (Channel NewsAsia), July 22, 2026
**Authors:** Nadine Regina Yeam, Sabrina Ng, Olivia Chang

**Key Content:**
- NHG Health (National Healthcare Group) has partnered with social service agency Thye Hua Kwan Moral Charities to expand the Mobile Inpatient Care @ Home (MIC@Home) programme
- 82-year-old Mr Jiang Tong Kai is a case example — admitted to Khoo Teck Puat Hospital but receiving treatment at home
- Caregivers (family) are trained by healthcare professionals; supported by home visits and charity workers
- The programme aims to expand hospital-level care delivery to more patients' homes and nursing homes
- Part of Singapore's broader strategy to shift care from hospitals to community settings
