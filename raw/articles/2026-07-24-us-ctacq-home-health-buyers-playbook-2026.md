# Buy a Home Health Business in 2026: 8-15x by Sub-Vertical, PDGM, CHOW Timeline

**Source:** CT Acquisitions
**Date:** Updated June 2026
**Key Themes:** M&A, home health, valuation multiples, PE roll-up

**Multiple bands by sub-vertical (2026):**

| Sub-vertical | EBITDA Multiple Range | Anchor Deal |
|---|---|---|
| Medicare-certified home health | 8x–12x | Enhabit/Kinderhook May 2026: 10.2x on $108M EBITDA |
| Non-medical home care | 7x–11x | TEAM Services/General Atlantic April 2026: ~10x for $3B |
| Hospice | 9x–15x+ | Bristol Hospice March 2026 auction: $140M EBITDA, $1B+ bids |

**Platform profiles and multiples:**
- Single-site <$5M EBITDA: 6.0–8.0x (CHOW execution risk priced in)
- $5–$15M EBITDA multi-site: 8.0–11.0x (case-mix discipline)
- $15–$50M regional platform: 9.0–12.0x (quality bonus uplift)
- $50M+ multi-state platform: 10.0–12.0x

**Seven buyer archetypes in home health:**
1. National PE platforms (pay highest multiples, 10x+ for $15M+ targets)
2. Strategic acquirers (Optum/Amedisys, Humana/CenterWell, Addus, Pennant, BrightSpring)
3. Health system JVs (Compassus model: 8–11x + strategic premium)
4. Independent sponsors & lower-middle-market PE
5. Search funds ($1–$3M EBITDA range, 5–7x)
6. Family offices
7. International buyers

**Key regulatory diligence factors:**
- PDGM 30-day episode case mix
- EVV vendor lock-in
- CMS 855A CHOW timeline (typically 4–9 months)
- CON state friction
- HOPE assessment replacing HIS (October 2025)
- VBID hospice carve-in sunset (December 2025)

**Demographic tailwinds:** 65+ population crosses 62M in 2026, reaches 73M by 2030. CMS projects home health spending to grow from $135B (2023) to $220B+ by 2031.

**Market structure:** >11,400 Medicare-certified home health agencies, >33,000 non-medical home care agencies, ~5,800 hospice providers. Top 10 players control <30% of certified episodes.
