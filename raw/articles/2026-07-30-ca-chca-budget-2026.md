The Canadian Home Care Association (CHCA) 2026 pre-budget submission outlines key federal investment priorities to strengthen Canada's home and community care sector. Recommendations support a coordinated, nationwide approach to improving access, affordability, workforce stability, and quality in home care. Key asks include sustained federal funding for home care, workforce training investments, and support for technology adoption.
