# Residential Care Homes (Elderly Persons) (Amendment) Regulation 2026 gazetted

**Source:** HKSAR Government Press Release, June 5, 2026

**Key Content:**
- New professional rank: **Health and Care Practitioners (HCPs)** established
- Registration system for HCPs; incorporated into staffing requirements for RCHs
- RCH operators can employ either enrolled nurses OR HCPs to meet statutory staffing requirements
- Basis of calculation changed from total beds to total residents (aligning with international practice)
- SWD to implement 3-year subsidy scheme for serving registered health workers to undergo HCP professional diploma
- Aim: attract people to social welfare sector, provide career progression, reduce competition for nursing talent with healthcare sector
- Amendment Regulations tabled at LegCo June 10; effective **October 14, 2026**
