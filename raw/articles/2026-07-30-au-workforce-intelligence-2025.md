Australian Government Department of Health and Aged Care official report. The health workforce is growing across Australia. The compound annual growth rate (CAGR) for nurses is projected to be 2.5% through the forecast period. Key challenge: workforce shortages in regional and remote areas, with aged care and home care experiencing most acute pressure. Healthcare and social assistance employs over 17% of the Australian workforce and is the fastest-growing industry sector.
