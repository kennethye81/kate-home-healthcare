# MOH to revise salary guidelines for community care staff; annual wages may rise by 7% or more

**Source:** The Straits Times, February 17, 2026
**Author:** Zhaki Abdullah

**Key Content:**
- 23,000 community care workers to see ~7%+ salary increase under revised guidelines by end-2026/early 2027
- MOH providing initial $100M to defray costs
- In addition to previously announced increase in max subsidy for residential long-term care (80% from July 2026)
- Follows 63,000 public sector healthcare professionals getting 7% increase from July 2025
- Healthcare Services Employees' Union (HSEU) advocated for narrowing gap between community care and acute care salaries
- Ong Ye Kung: "make healthcare an attractive career for every part of healthcare"
