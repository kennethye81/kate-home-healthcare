# The 2026-27 Budget — Caring for the Elderly

**Source:** Hong Kong Government Budget Speech, February 25, 2026

**Key Content:**
- CCSV: Increase by 4,000 to 16,000 (from current 12,000)
- RCSV: Increase by 1,000 to 7,000 (from current 6,000)
- Full-year expenditure: $1.2B for CCSV, $1.97B for RCSV
- Cross-boundary elderly care: 26 RCHEs across all 9 GBA cities
- Pilot Medical Subsidy Arrangement: RMB10K/year outpatient, RMB30K/year inpatient
- Portable cash assistance: Guangdong/Fujian Scheme participants can receive via Mainland bank accounts
- Working Group on Silver Economy: 30 measures announced, most with positive outcomes
