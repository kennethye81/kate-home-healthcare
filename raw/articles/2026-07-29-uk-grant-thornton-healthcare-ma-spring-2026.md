# Healthcare and Pharma Services M&A Review: Spring 2026

**Source:** Grant Thornton UK (Peter Jennings, Jessica Sandercock)
**Published:** Mid-2026

## Key Data
- **Q1 2026 deals:** 114 (37% uplift vs Q1 2025)
- **PE/VC-backed deals:** Up 43% vs Q1 2025
- **Social care deal:** Sovereign Capital acquired Apollo Home Healthcare (complex care provider, adults and children nationally) — financial due diligence by Grant Thornton

## Key Trends
1. **Diversification from NHS:** Providers using acquisition to diversify revenue streams (corporate health, private provision, overseas markets) due to funding constraints and right-to-choose restrictions
2. **PE buy-and-build consolidation:** Fragmented UK healthcare IT and MedTech services landscape creates arbitrage (buy at 4-6x EBITDA, consolidate into platform at 10-14x)
3. **MedTech:** Investors backing companies helping NHS transform from analogue to digital (one of 3 shifts in 10-Year Health Plan)
4. **MedTech deals:** G Square acquired Wellola (patient engagement) + CLAI (NHS AI-Scribe) via Mayden; Rockpool acquired CareScribe; Emerald secured pre-seed for GP-led preventative care
5. **Medical products:** Pennamed (endoscopic consumables) sold to Getinge; AddLife acquired BioSpectrum; GBL acquired controlling stake in Rayner Surgical Group
6. **Pharma services:** CBPE Capital invested in Access Infinity; BGF £15M in Seda Pharma; Bridgepoint-backed Prescient acquired Dolon
