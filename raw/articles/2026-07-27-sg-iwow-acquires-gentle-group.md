# S'pore firm behind alert buzzer for seniors to acquire eldercare social enterprise for $11.2m

**Source:** The Straits Times, May 1, 2026
**Author:** Anjali Raguraman

**Key Content:**
- iWOW Technology (SGX-listed) acquires The Gentle Group for S$11.2M
- The Gentle Group: social enterprise providing therapeutic meals (dysphagia, kidney disease, diabetes), rehabilitation solutions
- Services hospitals, nursing homes (Vanguard Healthcare, Methodist Welfare Services, Orange Valley)
- iWOW known for "Buddy of Parents" emergency buzzer system deployed nationwide
- **Order book: S$127 million** as of July 1, 2026 (initial 3-year term + 3-year extension option)
- Plans to scale production by 5x
- Strategic positioning as integrated data-driven platform for ageing societies
- SEEDS Capital (EDB/Enterprise SG arm) is existing shareholder of The Gentle Group
