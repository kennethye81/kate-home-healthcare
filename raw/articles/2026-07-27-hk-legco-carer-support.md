# LegCo Panel on Welfare Services — Services Supporting the Carers (June 8, 2026)

**Source:** Legislative Council Paper CB(1)570/2026(05)

**Key Content:**
- "District Services and Community Care Teams" scheme: extended to all 18 HK districts (April 2025)
- As at end-April 2026: visited/contacted **110,400+ households**, made **13,700+ welfare referrals**, assisted **3,600+ households** with emergency alarm system (Safety Bell) subsidies
- Scheme extended another 3 years from April 2026; target: **60,000+ households**
- **Support for Carers Project:** 450+ property management companies, 4,140+ buildings, 14,200+ trained PMP
- **Information Gateway for Carers:** 7.96M+ page views; AI Chatbot enhancement underway
- **Designated Hotline 182 183:** 24/7 manned by professional social workers
- **Smart Accident Detection Pilot Scheme:** 500 elderly households, sponsored by HKEX Foundation
  - Devices: fall detection, prolonged stillness, smoke/water leak detection
  - 52 applications received as of end-April 2026
  - Evaluation by Hong Kong Shue Yan University
