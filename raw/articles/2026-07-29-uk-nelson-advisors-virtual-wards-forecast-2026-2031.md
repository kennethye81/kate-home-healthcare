# The Global Evolution of Virtual Wards: Five Year Forecast and Strategic Assessment of the NHS, Continental Europe and the United States (2026–2031)

**Source:** Nelson Advisors (Healthcare Technology Investment Banking)
**Published:** 2026-05-27

## Summary
Comprehensive strategic assessment of virtual ward evolution across NHS, Europe, and US.

## Key NHS Strategic Roadmap (2026–2031)

| Year | Milestone |
|:----:|:----------|
| 2026 | NHS Shared Business Services launches standardised "Virtual Wards and Hospital at Home" procurement framework (eliminate regional vendor fragmentation) |
| 2027 | "NHS Online" — entirely virtual online hospital without physical site |
| 2028 | NHS App as primary digital front door (remote consultations, prescription fulfilment, diagnostic booking, single patient record) |
| 2031 | Double virtual ward capacity to 40 beds per 100,000 GP-registered patients; transition 2/3 of outpatient appointments to digital, saving £14 Bn/year |

## NHS 2025/26 Operational Planning
- Devolved financial autonomy to local ICSs (less ringfencing, more local flexibility)
- Four critical priority areas: ambulance reduction/front-door standardisation/LOS management/neighbourhood health model
- Virtual wards as primary "step-down" mechanism via Better Care Fund
- NHS organisations legally required to invest min 3% of annual budgets on digital transformation

## Key Statistics
- By March 2025: 20 virtual ward beds per 100,000 GP-registered patients across all 42 ICSs
- Treating 9,000+ acute patients daily
- Government 10-year plan "Fit for the Future": care hierarchy — digital by default, home if possible, neighbourhood centre when needed, hospital only when necessary
