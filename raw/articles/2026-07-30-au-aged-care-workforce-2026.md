The aged care sector is a cornerstone of Australia's healthcare system. Demand for skilled and compassionate professionals is rising. Home care employment trends in 2026 show that more Australians prefer staying at home, supported by professional home care workers, creating new career opportunities and pathways. Healthcare Australia's 2026 Salary Guide highlights persistent shortages in mental health, regional hospitals, aged care, and disability services. Organisations are shifting toward permanent contracts, community-based roles, and psychosocial safety plans.
