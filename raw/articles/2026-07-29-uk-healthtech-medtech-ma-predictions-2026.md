# UK HealthTech & MedTech Market 2026: Mergers, Acquisitions and Fundraising Predictions

**Source:** Nelson Advisors / Healthcare.Digital
**Published:** 2025-12-08 (updated)

## Summary
2026 is a maturation point for UK HealthTech/MedTech. Post-pandemic speculative excess replaced by strategic necessity (pharma patent cliff, clinical validation focus).

## Key Predictions
- Global M&A deal flow projected to reach **$3.9 Trillion** in 2026
- UK PE dry powder: ~$2.5 Trillion globally, over $1 Trillion held by US investors
- **Valuation discipline:** Clinically validated AI assets: 6x–8x revenue; general HealthTech without clinical ROI: 4x–6x revenue
- **Regulatory triple-lock:** NHS 10-Year Plan + MHRA SaMD roadmap + Mansion House Reforms = de-risks investment in high-growth verticals
- **Pharma patent cliff:** $180–400 Bn annual revenue at risk (2026–2030) driving aggressive M&A
- **"Buy and Build" dominant PE strategy:** Fragmented UK HCIT/MedTech consolidating via platform acquisitions
- **Care-at-home highlighted** by PwC as a key growth theme for H2 2026 M&A
