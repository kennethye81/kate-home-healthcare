# HHCN+ Report: The Booming Hospital-At-Home Market's Big Winners

**Source:** HHCN, July 2023
**Author:** Joyce Famakinwa

## Key Evidence Cited
- Mount Sinai (2018, JAMA IM): LOS 3.2 vs 5.5 days; lower readmissions + ED visits
- Annals of Internal Medicine (2020): Costs 38% lower; patients less sedentary; lower 30-day readmissions
- Journal of Clinical Oncology (2021): In-home cancer care → 55% reduction in unplanned hospitalizations, 47% lower costs

## Barriers
1. Cultural norms / hospital gatekeeper resistance (Leff: "Rewiring health care delivery and attitudes — all of that is hard")
2. Reimbursement uncertainty — CMS Acute Hospital Care at Home waiver (2020-2024 extensions)

## Market Players Profiled

### Inbound Health
- Enablement platform for health systems + health plans
- Spun off from Allina Health
- Scaled to 6,000 patients across 350 disease states
- Average daily census: low 50s (quintupled from 5-10 in early years)
- Expanded to post-surgical care (orthopedics, bariatrics, hernia)

### Biofourmis
- Unicorn status ($1B+ valuation, $300M raise in 2022)
- Founded Singapore 2015, US HQ Boston
- Two verticals: Biofourmis Care (care delivery) + pharma
- ~60 health system partnerships (from 7 in first year)
- Virtual command center model with phlebotomy, infusion, DEM

### Current Health (acquired by Best Buy)
- Holds ~25% of US hospital market
- Best Buy/Geek Squad provides "final mile" tech support into patient homes
- Platform: remote care management, telehealth, patient engagement
- CEO McGhee: "We as a society have spent trillions building hospital infrastructure... we have to collectively build up HaH infrastructure"
