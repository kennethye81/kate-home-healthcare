# DispatchHealth, Saint Francis Health System Launch New Hospital-at-Home Program

**Source:** HHCN, February 2026
**Author:** Morgan Gonzales

## Key Points
- DispatchHealth + Saint Francis Health System (Tulsa, OK) launch joint venture HaH program
- Saint Francis: Catholic not-for-profit, 12,000+ employees
- Opt-in program, initially staffed for 5-6 patients, virtual unit capacity ~40 patients
- Two Saint Francis campuses involved
- 30-50 patients/day eligible for HaH across Saint Francis system
- 20-mile radius around Tulsa metro (~30-min drive for care teams)
- Teams visit minimum twice daily; provide meals, personal care, 24/7 monitoring

## Quotes
- Meg Zacks (Saint Francis CSO): "Low capital investment vs. new bed towers... extend our mission into the home"
- Pippa Shulman (DispatchHealth CMO): "All the things you get in brick-and-mortar hospital, you get in the comfort of your own home"

## Context
- DispatchHealth operates across 20 states
- Merged with Medically Home (June 2025)
- Restructured operations Sept 2025 (exited 1 market, changed 9 others)
- 60% of Saint Francis patients from outside Tulsa County → long-term rural expansion vision
