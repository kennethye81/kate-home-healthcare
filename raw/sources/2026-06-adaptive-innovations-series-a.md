# Adaptive Innovations Lands $50M Series A

> AI-powered home health provider emerges from stealth with $60M total funding.
> Source: Home Health Care News + D CEO Magazine + Axios Pro (multiple coverage, June 2026)

**Key facts:**
- Series A: $50M led by Felicis, with Bain Capital Ventures, Optum Ventures, Sunflower Capital, Conviction, BoxGroup, Dorm Room Fund, Constellation
- Total raised: $60M (includes $10M unannounced seed in 2025)
- Headquarters: Dallas, Texas
- Employees: 400 (300 in Texas)
- Clinicians: 200
- Patient visits: 100,000+ in 18 months
- Partner organizations: 500+ across Texas
- Rehospitalization rate: <5% (industry avg: 11%)
- Documentation time reduction: 80% via AI-native workflows
- Key payer relationship: Leading Blue Cross Blue Shield Texas home health provider

**Business model:** Full-stack AI-native home healthcare provider — not a software vendor. Intake, scheduling, clinical documentation, and billing unified on proprietary AI platform.

**Team background:** Engineers from Palantir, Jane Street; operators from McKinsey, Harvard, Stanford, US Army Rangers. Co-founders: Alex Wendland, Logan Stinson, Ryan Tolsma, Hunter Stinson.
