# Hospital-at-Home Reduces In-Hospital Mortality, ED Visits – But Not Readmissions

**Source:** HHCN, May 2026 (reporting on JAMA Network Open study)
**Author:** Morgan Gonzales

## Key Findings (JAMA Network Open)
- Study: ~16,000 Medicare FFS beneficiaries at HaH-waivered hospitals (Jan 2021 – Dec 2022)
- ~4,200 received HaH services vs ~11,700 traditional inpatient

| Outcome | HaH | Inpatient |
|:--------|:---:|:---------:|
| In-hospital mortality | **0.4%** | 3.6% |
| ED visits (30-day) | **8.8%** | 10% |
| Readmissions (30-day) | 11.7% | 11% (no significant difference) |
| ICU escalation | **3.5%** | 7.9% |

- Total 30-day spend: modest reductions for HaH
- HaH admissions concentrated in US Northeast and South
- Authors: "HaH can deliver safe, efficient, alternative acute care while alleviating pressures on health care systems"
