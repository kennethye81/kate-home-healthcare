# Hospital at Home: How China's Push for In-Home Medical Care Actually Works

**Source:** CGTN, 2026-06-14
**Author:** Xu Hua (徐华)

## Summary
CGTN feature report on China's trial "Guidelines for Home Hospital Bed Services" (家庭病床服务), released approximately May 2026 by the National Health Commission.

## Key Points
- Policy brings hospital-level care to patients who cannot easily access hospitals: disabled, elderly, and those with chronic conditions
- Community healthcare stations are the primary delivery point for home hospital bed services
- Features Dr. Cai Yongkang, Deputy Director of Dachan Community Healthcare Station, Shenzhen Nanshan Medical Group Headquarters
- Doctors and nurses provide medical care and train family members in basic patient care at home
- Highlights the practical challenges: stairs without elevators for fracture patients, wound checks at home

## Context
- China's "9073" senior care model: 90% home-based, 7% community-based, 3% institutional
- This policy aligns with China's broader push for community-based healthcare and aging-in-place
- 15-minute medical service circle now covers 90%+ of China's residents (NHC March 2026)
