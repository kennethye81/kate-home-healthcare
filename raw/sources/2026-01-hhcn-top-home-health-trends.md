# Top Home Health Trends for 2026

**Source:** Home Health Care News (HHCN), January 2026
**Author:** Morgan Gonzales

## Six Key Trends

### 1. A Year of Layoffs
- Bayada laid off 10% of HQ staff (June 2025)
- DispatchHealth scaled back 10 markets (Sept 2025)
- Medicare rate cuts continue to pressure workforce

### 2. Resisting 'Enshittification'
- AI tools must prioritize human touch, not replace it
- Risk: automation making care feel soulless
- "AI should enable more meaningful human connections between caregivers and patients"

### 3. TEAM (Transforming Episode Accountability Model)
- CMS mandatory bundled payment model effective Jan 1, 2026
- 743 hospitals participating
- Regional target price model — providers compete within region
- Pressure to send patients to affordable settings (home health)

### 4. End of the Pure-Play Provider
- 2025 CMS proposed record 9% cut → final cut softer but still painful
- Providers diversifying into hospice, other service lines
- Empath Health CEO: "Skilled home health already runs at an operating loss for many agencies"

### 5. Hospital-at-Home Atrophy
- Acute Hospital Care at Home waiver on short-term extensions
- Hospital Inpatient Services Modernization Act stalled
- Inbound Health shut down (Nov 2025) citing regulatory uncertainty
- "Innovation will stall, growth will slow or reverse"
- Waiver expires Jan 30 unless Congress acts

### 6. Blocking Vertical Vision
- Vertical integration slowing under current administration
- 2026 expected to be a year of retrenchment
