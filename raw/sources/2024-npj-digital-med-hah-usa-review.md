# The Hospital at Home in the USA: Current Status and Future Prospects

**Authors:** Jay A. Pandit, Jonathan B. Pawelek, Bruce Leff, Eric J. Topol
**Journal:** npj Digital Medicine, 2024;7:48
**Published:** 2024-02-27

## Abstract
Annual cost of hospital care in US has risen to over $1 trillion despite worse outcomes vs. peer nations. HaH has made significant progress over two decades. Technological advancements in RPM, wearable sensors, HIT infrastructure, multimodal health data processing have contributed to rise. COVID-19 brought HaH into mainstream with reimbursement waivers. Reviews promises, challenges, and future potential.

## Key Points
- US hospital spending >$1T (2021), projected to increase
- Pre-COVID: HaH adoption scarce despite evidence (lack of perceived need, reimbursement barriers)
- COVID-19 waiver was catalyst for US adoption
- Technology enablers: RPM, wearables, AI, digital health platforms
- Global context: Australia and Norway have offered in-person HaH in most hospitals for 15+ years

Comprehensive review of US HaH landscape. Foundational reference piece covering history, technology, policy, and future outlook.
