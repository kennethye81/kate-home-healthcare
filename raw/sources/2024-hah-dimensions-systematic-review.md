# Dimensions and Components of Hospital-at-Home Care: A Systematic Review

**Authors:** Parniyan Nikmanesh, Jalal Arabloo, Hasan Abolghasem Gorji
**Affiliation:** Iran University of Medical Sciences, Tehran, Iran
**Journal:** BMC Health Services Research, 2024;24:1458
**PMCID:** PMC11587637 | Published: 2024-11-25

## Abstract
Hospital-at-home (HaH) care is a healthcare delivery approach providing acute care services at home as an alternative to traditional hospital care. This systematic review (PRISMA methodology) searched ScienceDirect, Scopus, PubMed, Cochrane Library, Web of Science, and Wiley Online Library from early 2000 to February 19, 2024.

## Key Results
- **Total articles retrieved:** 4,078
- **Articles included after screening:** 179
- **Dimensions/components identified:** 88 across 7 categories

### 7 Main Categories
1. **Benefits** — cost savings (n=30), patient/family satisfaction (n=23), reduction in readmissions (n=13)
2. **Challenges and obstacles** — implementation barriers, regulatory, payment
3. **Facilitators** — technology, policy support, training
4. **Management-related factors** — coordination, protocols, quality
5. **Medical conditions** — suitable diagnoses, severity criteria
6. **Factors associated with patients/families** — preferences (n=12), education needs
7. **Factors associated with caregivers** — training, support, burden
