# More Patients in Singapore to Receive Hospital-Level Care at Home and in Nursing Homes

**Source:** Channel News Asia (CNA), July 2026
**Authors:** Nadine Regina Yeam, Sabrina Ng, Olivia Chang

## Key Points

### MIC@Home Expansion
- MOH expanded MIC@Home to all government restructured hospitals (except IMH)
- Following 2022 pilot → 2024 expansion
- ~152 patients across KTPH, Tan Tock Seng Hospital, Woodlands Hospital
- Expected 150 more by end of 2026
- Currently free; THKMC exploring paid version + expansion to other clusters

### NHG Health + THKMC Partnership (July 2024)
- Social service agency Thye Hua Kwan Moral Charities provides 60 care staff
- Daily visits for 5 days post-discharge: vitals, medication, wound care, caregiving
- Hospital doctors/nurses/AHP oversee via home visits + teleconsultations

### THRIVE Program (Nursing Home)
- Woodlands Hospital pilot at Ren Ci @ Woodlands nursing home
- Geriatrician + advanced practice nurse + community nurse team
- 300 residents, 1-year pilot
- Results: unplanned hospital admissions ↓33%, ED visits ↓33%
- 60%+ of residents did not need hospital admissions during period
- Plans to expand to more nursing homes
