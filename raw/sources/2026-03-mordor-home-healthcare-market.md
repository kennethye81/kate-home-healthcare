# Home Healthcare Market to Expand at 8%+ CAGR by 2031

**Source:** Mordor Intelligence via GlobeNewswire, March 26, 2026

## Key Market Data
- Global market 2025: **USD 335.22 billion**
- Global market 2026: **USD 363.58 billion**
- Global market 2031: **USD 545.69 billion**
- CAGR (2026-2031): **8.46%**

## Regional Breakdown
- **North America**: Largest share — advanced infrastructure, high healthcare spending, strong digital health adoption
- **Europe**: Notable position — aging demographics, structured systems, reducing hospital stays
- **Asia-Pacific**: **Fastest-growing** — expanding infrastructure, rising awareness, affordable care demand (China, India, Japan)

## Key Drivers
1. Rising adoption of remote patient monitoring and telehealth
2. Growing demand for cost-effective and personalized care
3. Aging population and chronic disease prevalence
4. Favorable reimbursement policies and government initiatives
